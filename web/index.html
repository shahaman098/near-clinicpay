One invoice, every payer settled.

ClinicPay splits a clinic bill between the patient and the insurer, records the insurer's decision on chain, and pays the clinic directly. Nothing is held in between.

Split between payers

One invoice carries a share per payer: the patient's co-pay and the insurer's portion. Each pays their own share, in any order.

Insurer decisions on chain

When an insurer approves less than billed, the reason and the new amount are signed on chain and the difference moves to the next payer.

Gasless stablecoin payments

Payers settle in a stablecoin by signing a message. A relayer covers the network fee, so they never need to hold NEAR.

How it works

From bill to settled in four steps, every one checkable on chain.

  1. Clinic issues the invoice

    Line items, a share for each payer, and an optional due date go into the contract.

  2. Insurer adjudicates

    The insurer approves, reduces or denies its share with a reason. The shortfall moves to the patient's share.

  3. Payers settle

    Each share is paid from a wallet or gaslessly. Funds are forwarded to the clinic in the same flow.

  4. Everyone can verify

    The dashboard and invoice page read straight from the chain, including that ClinicPay holds zero.
